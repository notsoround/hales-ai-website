## Full description (Play Store limit 4,000 characters)

Cupcake is a food log and daily check-in that works the way you actually eat: point your camera at the plate and it's done.

**Snap it and you're done**
Open the camera, frame your plate and tap. Cupcake estimates the whole meal (calories, protein, carbs and fat) and logs it with the time you ate. Change the portion you actually finished with one tap: a quarter, half, three quarters or the full plate. The original estimate stays on record.

**Your day on one dial**
Today opens on a single dial: calories left against your target, how long since your last logged meal, and your latest glucose reading and last night's sleep when you have them. Tap any ring for the details behind it. Protein, carbs and fat sit right underneath.

**Charts you can touch**
Drag across your intake, glucose and weight charts to read any day. Weight shows a 7-day average so one salty dinner doesn't ruin your week.

**A coach who talks back**
Ask what to eat for dinner, how your week is going or how to plan tomorrow. Type, use live voice, or record a thought and come back to it later. Cupcake has opinions; switch to Matthieu for a calmer voice. Suggested changes are never saved until you tap to confirm.

**Your connections, in one place**
On iPhone, send steps, sleep, weight and more from Apple Health through the Health Auto Export app. Weigh-ins and glucose readings you enter yourself count the same.

**Share with your coach, on your terms**
Working with a coach? Share a clean report or your meal photos for a set time, and take access back whenever you like.

**Made for your phone**
Install it to your home screen and it opens like an app: full screen, Back closes what you opened, a light theme for outdoors, and a quiet line when you lose signal.

**Private by design**
Your account is yours. Download a copy of your data or close your account from Settings.

*Cupcake gives estimates to help you notice patterns. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Talk to a qualified professional about your health.*
